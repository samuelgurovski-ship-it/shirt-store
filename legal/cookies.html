What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies (like local storage) are small files saved by your browser. Some are needed for the site to work; others help us understand how it's used.

Your choices

Essential storage is always on because the cart won’t work without it. Analytics only run if you choose “Accept analytics.” You can change your choice at any time using , or by clearing your browser’s site data.
