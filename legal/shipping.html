Where we ship, how long it takes, and what it costs.

Processing time

Orders ship within 1–2 business days (Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays). During launches and sales it can take up to 5 business days. You'll get an email with tracking when your order ships.

Customs, duties and taxes

International orders may be charged import duties and taxes by the destination country. Unless checkout says these are included, they're your responsibility. If you refuse a package, we'll refund the order minus the original shipping and any return charges.

Address accuracy

Please check your address at checkout. If a parcel is returned to us because of an incorrect address, we'll reship it once you pay the new shipping cost. Email us within 12 hours of ordering if you need to change your address.

Lost or delayed parcels

If tracking hasn't updated in 7 days, or it says delivered but you can't find it, email us. We'll work with the carrier and, if it's lost, send a replacement or refund.
