Return window

You can return items within 30 days of delivery for a refund or exchange. If you're in the EU or UK, you also have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of delivery without giving a reason; our 30-day window covers this.

Condition of items

Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have their original tags. We may reduce the refund if an item shows wear beyond what's needed to try it on.

Final-sale items are marked on the product page and can't be returned unless faulty.

How to return

  1. Email hello@example.com with your order number and the items you're returning.
  2. We'll reply with a return address, and a prepaid label for US orders.
  3. Pack the items securely and send them back within 14 days of your request.

Return shipping costs

US returns: [free / $6 deducted from refund]. International returns are at your expense. If the item is faulty or we sent the wrong one, we'll cover return shipping everywhere.

Refunds

We inspect returns within 5 business days of receiving them and refund to your original payment method. Your bank may take another 5–10 business days to show it. Original shipping costs are refunded only if the whole order is returned within the EU/UK 14-day cancellation period or the item was faulty.

Exchanges

Need a different size? Mention it in your return email. We'll ship the new size as soon as the original is on its way back, subject to stock.

Faulty or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, faulty, or isn't what you ordered, email us within 30 days with photos. We'll replace or refund it at no cost. This doesn't affect your statutory rights.
