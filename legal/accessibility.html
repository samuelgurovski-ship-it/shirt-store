We want everyone to be able to shop here, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, or other assistive technology.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. The site supports keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader labels, reduced-motion settings, and text resizing.

Known limitations

Some third-party tools, like payment pages, are run by other companies and may not fully meet this standard. We're working with them where we can.

Tell us about a problem

If something on the site is hard to use, email hello@example.com. Tell us the page and what happened, and we'll reply within 2 business days and help you complete your order another way if needed.
