Why a heavier white tee

Thin white shirts go sheer, twist at the side seams, and stretch out at the collar within a season. Ours use denser jersey, a taped neck seam, and side seams that are cut straight, so the shirt hangs the same after its fiftieth wash as its first.

Buy them in packs and you'll always have a clean one in the drawer.
